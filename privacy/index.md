# Privacy Policy for Practice - Meditation timer

**Last Updated:** August 2, 2026

## 1. About This Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Practice - Meditation timer
(“Practice”, the “App”) or visit its privacy and support pages (together, the “Services”). It also
explains the choices available to you.

Practice is designed to be local-first. The App does not require an account with us and we do not
operate an application backend that receives your meditation data. Some features use Apple services,
including a private iCloud database, and the public privacy and support pages are hosted by GitHub.
Those providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

## 3. Summary of Our Privacy Practices

- We do not require a developer-operated account.
- We do not operate an application backend that receives your meditation data.
- We do not sell personal information or use it for advertising or tracking.
- We do not include third-party advertising or analytics SDKs in the App.
- We do not access Apple Health or HealthKit data, location, contacts, photos, or the microphone.
- Meditation data is stored locally and, when iCloud is available, selected data is synchronized
  through your private iCloud database.
- Diagnostic and data-export packages are created locally. Nothing is sent to us automatically;
  you choose whether and where to share a package.
- We receive personal information only if you choose to contact us, such as by sending an email or
  attaching a diagnostic package.

## 4. Information Stored on Your Device

Depending on how you use Practice, the App may store the following information locally on your
device:

- an optional profile display name and a selected built-in avatar identifier;
- completed meditation history, including dates, durations, outcomes, pauses, interruptions, and
  the session settings needed to represent the completed practice;
- custom preset names, phase names, phase durations, ordering, cue choices, and audio preferences;
- application preferences, such as display, haptic, sound, ambient-audio, and alarm-backup choices;
- active-session and recovery state needed to keep the timer reliable across interruption,
  backgrounding, and relaunch;
- technical identifiers and synchronization state created by the App; and
- a bounded local diagnostic journal described below.

This information is used to provide the timer, restore interrupted sessions, show history and
statistics, apply your settings, and support reliable synchronization. We do not receive this
on-device information automatically.

## 5. Information Stored in Your Private iCloud Database

If you are signed in to iCloud and iCloud is available, Practice uses Apple CloudKit to synchronize
selected information across devices using the same Apple Account. The synchronized information may
include:

- profile display name and built-in avatar identifier;
- completed meditation history and the related dates, durations, outcomes, and session plan;
- custom presets, preset and phase names, durations, and preset ordering;
- signal and background-audio defaults; and
- technical synchronization information such as record identifiers, revisions, timestamps,
  deletion markers, and conflict/recovery state.

Practice uses the private CloudKit database associated with your Apple Account. Apple states that
the user owns the contents of this database and that private-database contents are not visible in
the developer portal. The information counts toward your iCloud storage and is processed by Apple
under Apple’s privacy policy and iCloud terms.

CloudKit may use silent background notifications to keep the local replica current. Practice does
not use these notifications to display alerts, reminders, or marketing messages.

## 6. Diagnostics and Data Export

### Local diagnostics

Practice keeps a bounded, device-local diagnostic journal for reliability and support. It retains
at most the most recent 120 hours of technical events and is also limited by event and storage-size
bounds.

A diagnostic package may contain:

- App version, build, configuration, and distribution-channel information;
- operating-system version and device family;
- the configured CloudKit environment;
- coarse runtime, persistence, backup-readiness, and synchronization status;
- recent typed technical events and reason codes; and
- a random, resettable diagnostic installation identifier and report identifier.

The package is designed not to contain profile content, meditation history, preset names, audio,
Apple Account identifiers, Apple device identifiers, session or CloudKit record identifiers, file
paths, raw provider errors, or persistence payloads.

Nothing is uploaded automatically. You must explicitly prepare a report and then choose a
destination in the system share sheet. Locally prepared diagnostic exports are eligible for
automatic cleanup after 24 hours. Using **Clear Diagnostic History** removes the local diagnostic
events and resets the random diagnostic installation identifier without deleting meditation
history.

### Portable data export

The App can create a portable backup containing your profile, completed history, custom presets,
and supported settings. The archive is created locally. You decide whether and where to share or
store it using Apple’s system interfaces. Copies outside the App are controlled by you and by the
service or recipient you select; Practice cannot delete those external copies for you.

## 11. Retention and Deletion

- **On-device information:** retained until you edit or delete it using available App controls or
  remove the App. Removing the App deletes its local container through the operating system, but it
  does not by itself delete data already stored in iCloud, copies you exported elsewhere, or data
  retained in an Apple-managed device backup. Apple backup retention is controlled through your
  Apple settings and Apple’s terms.
- **Private iCloud information:** retained in your private CloudKit database until it is deleted
  using **Delete Cloud Data** or otherwise removed through Apple’s iCloud controls. **Delete Cloud
  Data** removes the current synchronized cloud workspace, keeps the data stored on the current
  device, and pauses synchronization. Selecting Retry later may republish the remaining local data.
  To remove both copies using the App’s current controls, first delete the cloud data, do not restart
  synchronization, and then remove the App from the device.
- **Local diagnostic journal:** retained for no more than 120 hours and subject to additional event
  and size limits. You may clear it sooner in the App.
- **Prepared diagnostic exports:** eligible for local cleanup after 24 hours. A copy you share or
  move elsewhere follows the retention rules of the selected destination.
- **Support correspondence:** retained for no more than 90 days after the request is closed, unless
  a longer period is required to comply with law or establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.
- **Website technical information:** retained by GitHub according to GitHub’s own retention
  practices; we do not add or operate a visitor-analytics service for the static pages.

No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You should review the
destination before sharing a backup or diagnostic package and protect exported files according to
their sensitivity.
